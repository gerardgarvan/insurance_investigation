# 192.1 ICD-9 Mapping — CMS 2018 GEM Verification

**Source files:** `docs/reference/2018_I10gem.txt` (81,593 lines), `docs/reference/2018_I9gem.txt` (24,860 lines)
**Method:** Forward GEM (ICD-10→ICD-9) to find ICD-9 targets of each in-scope ICD-10 family; reverse GEM (ICD-9→ICD-10) to confirm that each candidate ICD-9's targets fall within the requested ICD-10 families. Codes whose reverse targets fall entirely outside the requested families are excluded. Codes whose reverse targets include codes both inside and outside the request are labelled `broader`.
**Date:** 2026-10-29

All flags in the GEM files were `10000` (approximate, no combination) for every entry in this mapping.

## Notes for Config Constant

1. ICD-9 keys in `AUTOIMMUNE_DX_CODES$icd9`: use the codes without dots, uppercase, matching the PCORnet DX field format after `normalize_icd()`.
2. `4460` and `6944` must be labelled `broader` in the constant's comment.
3. The M313 note still stands: "M31.3C3 in the original request is not a valid ICD-10-CM code; read as M31.3 (M31.30 and M31.31 in ICD-10-CM). Assumption for team confirmation in Phase 194."
4. `71430` is shared by M08.00, M08.09, and M08.3 forward mappings. It appears once in the constant.
5. `6943` (pustular psoriasis / dermatitis herpetiformis): although ICD-9-CM lists this under "dermatitis herpetiformis", the GEM maps it to L40.1 (pustular psoriasis), which is within the L40 family. Included.
