<objective>
Characterize echocardiogram receipt relative to the last anthracycline dose (SRATE-03, SRATE-04). Define the echo clock start as the last doxorubicin (incl. liposomal, per Phase 164 DRUG_NAME_ALIASES) date within first-line episodes (180-day file, D-166-02), with a latest-ever sensitivity column (D-10). Compute: days to first echo strictly after last dose; cumulative incidence of first echo at 1/2/5 years via Aalen-Johansen (death competing event) alongside 1-KM (D-11, D-12); and echo rate per person-year from last dose to follow_end (D-14b, SRATE-04). Exclude patients with no anthracycline, no first-line episode, or no usable time after last dose, counting each in QC. Record D-166-01 (which anthracycline drugs appear).

Purpose: SRATE-03 + SRATE-04. The data-visibility caveat (echoes outside OneFlorida+ partner sites not captured; CIF/rates are lower bounds — D-14c) is carried into the KEY/C sheet by Plan 04.
Output: In-memory echo-block objects + C_anthracycline_echo summary + echo QC, handed to Plan 04 via a dated parts RDS; a reusable utils file; a testthat fixture file.
</objective>
