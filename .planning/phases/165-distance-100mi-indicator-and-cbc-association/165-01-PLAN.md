    # 165-METHODS.md: Distance >100 mi and CBC Association — Statistical Methods Memo

    **Phase:** 165-distance-100mi-indicator-and-cbc-association
    **Prepared:** [DATE]
    **Status:** Draft — awaiting D-165-01 (team method selection)

    ---

    ## Research Question

    Among Hodgkin Lymphoma patients in OneFlorida+, is living >100 miles from the
    treating facility associated with lower receipt of CBC (complete blood count)?

    ---

    ## Data and Definitions

    **Distance indicator:** `far_from_care_100mi` — binary (0/1), encounter level.
    Derived from `distance_mi` in the R/122 enc_distance tibble (haversine distance,
    patient ZIP centroid to facility ZIP centroid). Cutoff: 100 miles.

    **CBC event (locked, R/147):** WBC (LOINC 6690-2) + Hgb (718-7) + PLT (777-3)
    resulted on the same calendar date for the same patient. Post-anchor window only
    (anchor day = pre, per D-25).

    **Encounter scope:** All ENC_TYPE included. Telehealth/virtual encounters
    (ENC_TYPE TH/OA) carry a computed distance that may not reflect true travel burden;
    see QC counts below.

    **Clustering:** Multiple encounters per patient. Naive tests that ignore clustering
    understate standard errors if the intracluster correlation (ICC) is non-trivial.

    ---

    ## Candidate Tests

    | # | Method | Unit of Analysis | CBC Operationalization | Clustering handling | Effect size | Package |
    |---|--------|------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------------|---------|
    | 1 | Naive Pearson chi-square | Encounter | CBC on same date as encounter | None (assumes independence) | OR (cell counts) | base R |
    | 2 | Rao-Scott cluster-adjusted chi-square | Encounter | CBC on same date as encounter | Cluster on patient ID, DEFF correction | OR (svyglm) | survey |
    | 3 | GEE logistic regression (exchangeable) | Encounter | CBC on same date as encounter | Cluster on patient ID, sandwich SE | OR (marginal) | geepack |
    | 4 | Patient-level aggregate + chi-square/Fisher | Patient | Any CBC post-anchor | N/A (one row per patient) | OR (Fisher) | base R |

    ---

    ## Prototype Results (from 165_prototype_output.log)

    ## Covariate Caveat: SOURCE as Distance Proxy (D-11)

    SOURCE (contributing site) is likely a near-proxy for distance: patients from distant
    facilities are more often attributed to sites far from the cohort centroid. Adjusting
    for SOURCE in the sensitivity model may absorb much of the `far_from_care_100mi` effect,
    producing a biased (attenuated) adjusted OR. We present this covariate in the sensitivity
    model only, and flag this interpretation risk explicitly.

    ---

    ## Rurality Data Completeness (D-12)

    Rurality (RUCA/RUCC) is derived from ZIP codes in the SES work and has incomplete
    coverage. Patients with missing rurality are dropped from the adjusted sensitivity model.
    The QC sheet in the Plan 02 output will report N dropped.
