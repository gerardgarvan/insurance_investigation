    # Distance >100 mi and CBC: Statistical Methods Memo

    **Phase:** 165 · **Prepared:** [date] · **Status:** Draft — awaiting D-165-01

    ## Question
    Among HL cohort patients, is having an encounter more than 100 miles from the residence
    on file associated with CBC receipt?

    ## Definitions
    - far_from_care_100mi: encounter-level 0/1, ZIP-centroid distance (residence to encounter
      facility) > CONFIG$far_from_care_cutoff_mi (100 mi). All ENC_TYPE included.
    - CBC (locked, R/147): WBC 6690-2 + Hgb 718-7 + PLT 777-3 on the same date.
    - Encounter-level CBC: a CBC dated within the encounter (admit to discharge; admit date
      only for single-day encounters).
    - Patient-level CBC: any CBC in the window.
    - Windows: whole record; post-anchor (encounter date > HL anchor date; anchor day = pre).

    ## Why the test choice matters
    Patients contribute many encounters (distribution: [from log]). Encounters from the same
    patient are not independent, so a naive chi-square understates uncertainty when outcomes
    cluster within patients. The design effect quantifies by how much.

    ## Candidates
    | # | Method | Unit | CBC measure | Clustering | Effect size |
    |---|--------|------|-------------|------------|-------------|
    | 1 | Pearson chi-square | Encounter | CBC within encounter | Ignored | OR (Woolf) |
    | 2 | Rao-Scott chi-square (F) | Encounter | CBC within encounter | Patient clusters | OR (svyglm) |
    | 3 | GEE logistic | Encounter | CBC within encounter | Robust SE, cluster = patient | Marginal OR |
    | 4 | Patient-level chi-square / Fisher | Patient | Any CBC in window | Not applicable | OR (Fisher) |

    ## Results
    One table per window (whole record, post-anchor): OR, 95% CI, SE(log OR), DEFF, p, N.
    2x2 tables with suppress_display() applied to cells 1-10. Expected-cell check. GEE working
    correlation used (and why, if independence). Haldane correction flags, if any.

    ## Interpretation
    - What the DEFF values say about clustering in this cohort (state the numbers; no fixed cutoff).
    - Whether encounter-level and patient-level results agree in direction and size.
    - Whether the two windows differ, and a plausible reason if so.

    ## Caveats
    - SOURCE: encounters far from home tend to occur at a different SOURCE site than the
      patient's usual care, so SOURCE partly measures the same thing as distance. Adjusting for
      it in the sensitivity model may absorb much of the association; the adjusted estimate
      should be read with that in mind.
    - Rurality: incomplete ZIP coverage; the adjusted model drops encounters without it (N in Plan 02 QC).
    - Distances are ZIP-centroid straight-line distances, not travel distance or time.
    - Telehealth encounters carry a facility ZIP that may not reflect travel ([N] in analysis set).

    ## Recommendation
    One method for the primary analysis, with the reasoning tied to the numbers above, and the
    second-choice method named as the sensitivity check.

    ## D-165-01
    Team selection: ________  Date: ________
