### V2 — Insurance (same-day payer)
- Pipeline: `R/60_tiered_same_day_payer.R` → `payer_resolved_detail_all.csv` (output/tables/)

### V6 aggregation rules
- One death date per patient: `resolve_death_date()` applies D3 (source-priority then earliest consistent date)
- Last treatment date = max ADMIT_DATE across all treatment encounters in the pipeline output
  (to be derived independently from DuckDB ENCOUNTER scoped to treatment episode IDs)
- Patients with missing death date: counted in own row (death_flag = "no_death_record")
- Patients with missing last treatment date: counted in own row
