<objective>
Deliver Phase 181: a patient-level 2x2 chi-squared analysis of prior-year CBC receipt vs
far-from-care, and a one-page team summary memo closing the 2026-10-13 Erin/Ray request.

The analysis is READ-ONLY: it reads the pinned Phase 179 patient-year RDS
(`output/internal/cbc_patient_year_20261013.rds`) and does no new cohort building or exposure
derivation. "Prior year" = each patient's most recent fully observed follow-up year
(`max(year_index)` per patient). Primary set: patients whose most recent full year is
year_index >= 2 (survivorship). Secondary set: all patients (year_index >= 1).

Purpose: Answer "are far-from-care patients less likely to get a CBC in their most recent
follow-up year?" with a simple, team-facing 2x2 test, and summarize how it relates to the
earlier Phase 165 (encounter-level) and Phase 179 (patient-year GEE) results plus the Phase 180
robustness/capture checks.

Output:
- `R/181_cbc_prior_year.R` (new analysis script)
- `output/cbc_prior_year_<YYYYMMDD>.xlsx` (produced when the script is run on HiPerGator)
- `.planning/phases/181-cbc-prior-year-vs-distance/181-MEMO.md` (team memo)
- Registration in `R/39_run_all_investigations.R` and `R/SCRIPT_INDEX.md`
