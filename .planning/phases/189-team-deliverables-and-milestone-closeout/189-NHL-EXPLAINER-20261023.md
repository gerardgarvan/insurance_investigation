# NHL Subset Identification: Summary for Team Review

## Background

Patients in the Hodgkin Lymphoma (HL) cohort may have co-occurring non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL) diagnoses recorded in their treatment history. To support downstream analyses, patients were classified into two subsets based on the cancer-type designation assigned to each chemotherapy treatment episode.

## Classification Approach

Each chemotherapy row in the treatment episode file was evaluated against the cancer-type columns contributed by a left-join at the treatment-period level (one row per patient and episode number). Episodes were classified as "Definitely NHL", "Definitely HL", or "HL and NHL" based on the content of those columns. A patient was assigned to the NHL-only subset if all their episodes were classified as Definitely NHL and none were classified as HL or HL+NHL. A patient was assigned to the HL+NHL subset if any episode was classified as HL and NHL.

Non-chemotherapy rows in the episode file do not receive cancer-type designations and were not used in this classification.
