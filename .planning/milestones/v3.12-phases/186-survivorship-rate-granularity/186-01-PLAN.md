Purpose: deliver the RATE-02/RATE-03 team table — annual and biannual (D-186-01: biannual = every 6 months) survivorship rates per modality in one place, with annual values matching R/184 to the last digit, and no small count recoverable by arithmetic.

Output: `R/186_survivorship_rate_granularity.R` and (on HiPerGator run) `output/survivorship_rate_granularity_<date>.xlsx`.

No new patient-year computation. R/184 is not modified (read-only). All values carried verbatim from the pinned workbook, including suppressed "<11" strings; the complementary pass can only add suppression, never remove it.
