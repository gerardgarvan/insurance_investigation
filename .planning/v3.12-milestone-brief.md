### Survivorship modality rate granularity
- **RATE-01** — Patient-level count and rate per modality, annual window, read from the verified R/184 output (no recomputation drift; values read by exact label from the pinned workbook).
- **RATE-02** — "Biannual" definition resolved by the team (D-186-01) and implemented: (a) every 6 months — already the R/184 sensitivity tier, reuse it; or (b) every 2 years (biennial) — add a 2-year window.
- **RATE-03** — Single team-facing table: modality × window (annual / biannual) with patients, events, patient-years, rate; KEY sheet leftmost; UF colours.

### Anthracycline exposure
- **ANTH-01** — `any_anthracycline` (TRUE/FALSE) per cohort patient. Drug set (D-187-02): all anthracyclines present in the codeset / schema probe — doxorubicin (liposomal collapsed per Phase 164), daunorubicin, epirubicin, idarubicin; mitoxantrone excluded. Counts reported by flag and by drug.
- **ANTH-02** — One `last_anthracycline_date` per patient = latest administration date in the data (D-187-01). `years_since_last_anthracycline` = last dose → end of follow-up (last encounter or data cutoff, whichever is earlier). Each echo also carries days since that last dose. NA (not 0) for unexposed patients.
- **ANTH-03** — Both variables written to `output/internal/` at patient grain and joined into the survivorship analysis set; schema probe updated.

### Echocardiogram rate since anthracycline
- **ECHO-01** — Echo rate = echoes after the last anthracycline dose ÷ `years_since_last_anthracycline`, among `any_anthracycline == TRUE`. Echoes on or before the last dose (baseline / during treatment) are excluded and counted separately.
- **ECHO-02** — Reconciliation against the Phase 166 anthracycline-to-echo timing (D6/D7/D8 chain): report where first- vs last-administration anchoring changes counts.
